# YueJi brand assets

Icon: generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then exported as an opaque 1024 × 1024 RGB PNG for the iOS asset catalog. The editable animation counterpart uses independent native SwiftUI body, eyes, cheeks and sparkle layers in `MoonBuddy`.

Final generation prompt:

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished 1024x1024 opaque square iOS app icon for YueJi, a friendly personal expense and savings app. Full bleed deep midnight navy background, no rounded-square outer border (iOS clips it). Large original warm ivory crescent moon mascot, thick softly rounded crescent opening to the upper right, two small dark navy oval eyes and tiny curved smile on the lower central body, subtle coral cheeks. One small four-point golden sparkle floats inside the open upper-right crescent. Cheerful premium tactile 2.5D vector-like illustration, soft bevel and controlled glow, very clean silhouette readable at 40px, no clutter, no text, no letters, no currency symbols, no owl, no copied brand character. Centered generous 15 percent padding. Warm ivory with buttery golden lower edge against navy and a very subtle royal blue radial glow. Deliver only the square icon artwork.

Audio: original synthesized bell tones. Rebuild with `python3 scripts/generate_sounds.py`. No external soundtrack or sound sample used.

QA pending on iPhone: cold launch / warm return, silent switch, music playing, sound toggle, reduced motion, save response, goal reached and opening preview. Linux checks confirm source wiring and resource integrity; they do not verify native rendering or audio routing.
