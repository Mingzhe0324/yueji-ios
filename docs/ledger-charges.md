# Optional SST and service charges

New and existing ledger entries can opt into SST / Service Charge fields. Users enter the pre-charge subtotal and each percentage; no tax rate is inferred or preselected. Blank percentages mean zero. Rates accept 0–100 with up to two decimal places. Both charges independently use the subtotal and round separately to the source currency's minor unit. MYR100 + SST6% + service10% therefore yields MYR116, rather than compounding the rates.

The actual payment remains the existing gross Expense.cents (and ForeignAmount.minor where applicable). A separate, explicit “Use calculated payment total” action fills the payment field. Saving rejects any mismatch. Opening an automatic bank entry never infers a tax-exclusive subtotal, and reopening/editing an entry loads the stored fields without adding fees again. Unsaved fee edits participate in the editor's existing dismissal confirmation. Refund/repayment history locks fee edits; active settlements include charge metadata in their immutable terms.

Optional Expense.charges metadata stores source currency, subtotal and rates in basis points. Existing entries with no metadata retain their old amounts and item behavior. Backups, deletion/undo and edits preserve the metadata. CSV appends six charge columns in source currency; existing payment/report columns retain their meaning. Excluding a bank notice or converting a payment into a settlement cannot silently discard charge details.

Purchased items retain their tax-exclusive MYR unit prices and quantities. Their sum must equal the converted subtotal. Gross MYR payment is proportionally allocated across item base amounts using Decimal arithmetic, rounding down and distributing remaining sen in saved item order. These financial capacities are used for category reports, item ownership, refund allocations and personal net shares; the saved item prices are never rewritten to include fees. Existing whole-transaction splits remain proportional. Reimbursements continue to exclude the claimed part of the personal net share. Foreign subtotal and gross are converted using the same quote, once each; gross allocation absorbs conversion rounding. Item detail rows show the allocated charge separately.
